About Island Computing Services

ICS is building a new kind of cloud platform, Simplifying Computing for cloud-native and AI-native builders in India and beyond.

Our mission

Empower every builder with an opinionated cloud stack that keeps shipping simple, secure, and cost-efficient. We combine compute, data, and dev tools in one island so teams focus on outcomes, not integration.

Why now

India is on track to launch over 1 lakh cloud-native startups by 2030. They need sovereignty, integrated tooling, and AI-ready infrastructure without hyperscaler overhead. ICS exists to provide exactly that.
