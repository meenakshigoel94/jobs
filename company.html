About Mission

The team simplifying computing for India-first builders.

Island Computing Services was founded in Bengaluru to give the 1 lakh+ cloud-native and AI-native startups an opinionated platform that feels as calm as it is powerful.

Timeline

  • 2022 — Launched compute islands with managed networking.
  • 2023 — Added managed databases, CI/CD, and observability.
  • 2024 — Introduced Sovereign regions & AI runtimes.
  • 2025 — Expanded support teams to Delhi, Mumbai, and Singapore.
