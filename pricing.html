Pricing FAQ

Clear answers so you can budget confidently.

Do you charge for stopped islands?

Stopped islands only incur storage charges; compute pauses immediately.

How does egress work?

Intra-ICS traffic is free. External egress includes a 5% allowance then per-GB pricing.

Can I mix plans?

Yes. Compute and database tiers can be combined; billing is prorated.

Is support included?

Builder includes business hours support. Scale and Sovereign include 24/7 coverage.
