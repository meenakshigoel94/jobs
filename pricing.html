FAQ

Common questions on billing and support.

Do prices include support?

Yes. All paid tiers include business-hours support; 24/7 support is available on Scale and above.

Can I cap my spend?

Budgets and alerts are available for compute, database storage, and egress so you can set hard or soft limits.

How is data egress billed?

Egress is billed per GB with discounts for committed or high-volume usage. Traffic within ICS regions is free.

Is billing available in INR?

Yes. ICS prices default to INR, with optional USD billing for global subsidiaries operating in India.
